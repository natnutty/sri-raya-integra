SRI Consulting (SRI) is a Jakarta-based professional services firm providing accounting, tax, and financial advisory services to businesses across Indonesia.

Our team brings more than 30 years of combined experience — including Big Four and international practice — to every engagement, large or small.

Our mission is simple: give decision-makers the timely, reliable financial information they need to act with confidence.

And we measure every piece of work against one standard — integrity, and your complete satisfaction.
